What PPI measures

Every pitch changes the at-bat. A strike moves the batter into a worse count, a ball into a better one, and a ball in play has an expected result from its exit speed and angle. PPI measures how much each pitch lowered the batter's expected result, using what really happened to batters in that same count since 2023. It counts three things: the batter's overall expected result (xwOBA), his chance of reaching base (a walk counts), and his chance of hitting for power.

How to read the numbers

Each pitch is ranked against every pitch thrown in the same count, so a great 0-0 pitch and a great 3-2 pitch both score high. 100 is average and 10 points is one standard deviation: 110 beats about 84% of pitches, 120 about 98%. Season grades are adjusted for sample size, so a pitcher with few pitches is pulled toward 100.

The family of grades

PPI is the overall grade of the pitch. Arsenal+ grades only its physics: velocity, spin, movement, release and how it pairs with his fastball, ignoring where it went. Command+ grades how close he came to his usual spot for that pitch, hitter side and count, and how costly a likely ball was in that count. Projected PPI is 75% PPI plus 25% Arsenal+, the mix that best predicted the next season. Runs saved adds up how much better each pitch was than an average pitch in the same count, in runs.

Scouting scale

The scouting grade is PPI minus 50, rounded to the nearest 5 and kept between 20 and 80.

Where the data comes from

Pitch-by-pitch Statcast data from Baseball Savant, regular season and postseason, 2023 to 2026. Each season is graded by models trained only on the other seasons, so no grade comes from a model that saw that season.
